1. Scope

This policy applies when you visit the Sage website, submit a consultation request, register and use a Sage account, or use the Sage web service and the Sage app. Where an enterprise customer uses customized services under a separate contract, the data processing terms of that contract prevail; anything the contract does not cover is governed by this policy.

The Sage Services may link to third-party websites or services, such as the MIIT filing lookup or the Google sign-in page. Their processing of personal information is governed by their own rules, not this policy.

2. Information we collect and why

We collect only what is necessary for each feature. You may decline to provide information, but the related feature may then be unavailable.

2.1 Browsing the website

To keep the site secure and diagnose faults, our servers keep access logs: IP address, time of access, request method, page path (without query parameters), response status, data size and a request ID. We do not use advertising trackers or cross-site analytics.

2.2 Consultations and demos

When you submit the consultation form, we collect your company or brand name, phone number, work email (optional) and the business question you describe, to contact you, arrange a demo and answer your inquiry. Contact details and question content are deleted 90 days after the inquiry is completed.

2.3 Registration and sign-in

  • Your email address or phone number, and records of verifying the codes we send you, to confirm account ownership.
  • Your password. We store only an irreversible hash, never the password itself.
  • Bot protection: sign-in and registration pages use the Tencent Cloud CAPTCHA service, which collects device and network information as described in Tencent Cloud’s CAPTCHA personal information protection rules.
  • If you choose Google sign-in, we receive your Google account ID, email address and the name you authorize. This involves an overseas organization; see Section 6.

2.4 Account profile

Display name, avatar (re-encoded on upload with embedded image metadata removed, used only to display your account), notification preferences, and the versions of the Terms of Service and this policy you accepted, with the time of acceptance.

2.5 Account security

Sign-in sessions (a summary of your browser or device type, IP address and creation time), so you can review and sign out other devices in your settings; passkey public keys; and records of step-up verification for sensitive actions. We never receive biometric data: fingerprint or face verification happens locally on your device.

2.6 Business accounts

When a business admin invites members, they provide the invitee’s email or phone number. We use it to send the invitation and record member roles, seat assignments and permission changes for account management and audit.

2.7 Using AI services

Task descriptions you submit, materials you upload or authorize us to read, the analysis we generate and your task history, to provide the service you request, keep your results and handle your feedback. To generate results, this content is sent to the large-model providers that serve us (see Section 4). Please do not include unrelated personal information, keys, passwords or bank card details in a task.

2.8 Subscriptions and payments

Once paid features open, we record orders, amounts, payment status and invoice details (invoice title, tax ID and delivery email). Payments are processed by licensed payment institutions; we do not store full card numbers. When a payment provider is connected, we will add its name and processing scope to this policy and tell you before you pay.

2.9 Free trial

To make sure each person claims the free trial only once, after an account is deleted we keep an irreversible keyed hash of its sign-in identifier (email or phone number) for 12 months. The hash cannot be turned back into the original and is used only to check whether a trial has been claimed.

2.10 Contact and notifications

We send verification codes, security alerts, and order and account updates by email or SMS. You can turn off product update notifications in your settings at any time; security and transaction notices are necessary to provide the service and cannot be turned off.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use only cookies necessary for the site to work:

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in for up to 7 days and ends when you sign out.
  • Security token cookie: protects against cross-site request forgery.

Some pages use your browser’s session storage to hold interface state, such as the current tab or demo progress; it is cleared when you close the tab. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. You can clear or block cookies in your browser, but you will then be unable to sign in.

6. Cross-border transfer

We do not proactively provide your personal information outside mainland China. Only if you choose “Sign in with Google” does an overseas organization, Google LLC, become involved: you provide your account details directly to Google on Google’s page, and Google returns the sign-in result to us. We do not give Google any other personal information you hold in Sage. After Google returns, we create or link your Sage account only once you accept the Terms of Service and this policy on the confirmation page. Not using Google sign-in does not affect any other feature.

  • Overseas organization: Google LLC
  • Contact: https://support.google.com/policies
  • Purpose and method: verifying your Google account and returning the sign-in result to us
  • Information we receive from Google: your Google account ID, email address and the name you authorize
  • How to exercise your rights: revoke Sage’s access in your Google account settings, or contact us (Section 12) to unlink Google sign-in; Google’s processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy

7. Security

We protect your personal information with HTTPS encryption across the site, irreversible password storage, least-privilege access control for staff and systems with access auditing, logs that exclude query parameters and other content that may contain personal information, and regular database backups with tested restores.

No security measure on the internet is perfect. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will activate our response plan, take remedial action, and notify you by email, SMS or in-product notice of what happened, its possible impact, what we have done and what you can do, and report to the competent authorities as required.

8. Your rights

  • Access, copy and correct: view and edit your display name, avatar and notification preferences, and review signed-in devices in your settings. For other information, contact us for a copy or correction.
  • Delete: ask us to delete personal information when the purpose has been achieved, you withdraw consent, or we process it in breach of the law or our agreement.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off product update notifications or unlink Google sign-in. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.
  • Delete your account: request deletion in your settings. Complex accounts, such as those with open orders or a business admin role, are reviewed by our staff: we first respond within 2 working days and normally complete deletion within 7 working days. After deletion, your personal information is deleted or anonymized, except transaction records the law requires us to keep and the hash described in Section 2.9.
  • Portability: where the conditions set by the Cyberspace Administration are met, ask us to transfer your personal information to another processor you designate.
  • Explanation: ask us to explain this policy and our processing rules.

Send requests to privacy@sagegogo.com. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will respond within 15 working days of receiving your request and verifying your identity.

9. Minors

The Sage Services are intended for businesses and adults. Children under 14 may not register for or use the Sage Services; minors aged 14 to 17 should use them only with the consent and guidance of a guardian. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 14 without guardian consent, we will delete it promptly.

10. AI-generated content and automated decisions

Sage’s analysis is generated by digital employees from the materials you provide and public information, for your reference and decision. We do not make decisions with a significant effect on your personal rights based solely on automated processing. External actions such as price changes, purchases, publishing or payments run only after you authorize them, and you can withdraw authorization at any time.

For material changes to purposes, methods, types of information or recipients, we will also notify you in the product, by email or by SMS, and ask for your consent again where required.
