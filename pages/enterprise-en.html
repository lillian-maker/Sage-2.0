Business edition

One team, one set of rules, one body of company knowledge

People and digital specialists work in one workspace, while rules, access, knowledge and audit are managed by the business.

Everything waiting for you, in one place

Approvals, information requests, acceptance and invitations land in Needs my decision and stay on top; external messages pass rule and model filters before they reach the Inbox.

  • An approval covers only the targets, actions, scope and time on the card
  • Handle it in the Inbox or the task and both stay in sync
  • The notification center reminds you once, without repeating the queue

Team conventions become rules every task follows

Up to 30 rules of 200 characters each apply to every task in the workspace; when one conflicts with your current instruction, the specialist points it out first.

  • Every change creates a version with author and diff, and can be rolled back
  • Workspace admins edit; members can only read
  • For example: never price below cost plus 15%; compliance conclusions must cite the regulation

Share tasks and materials by role

Owner, editor, viewer and guest roles; guests see only the tasks they are invited to.

  • A business workspace is visible only to its creator until access is granted
  • A member’s edit rights never become a specialist’s execution rights
  • Personal workspace materials are never shared with the business automatically

Company materials with versions, access and status

Materials are scoped to a person, a workspace or the business; they can be cited only after indexing, and a citation pins the version.

  • The list shows source, indexing status and when each was last cited
  • Stale materials are marked expired and flagged when cited
  • Access and edit rights are set per library

Entities and facts from your materials, with conflicts flagged

Brands, products, markets, channels, competitors and regulations, each fact with its source, date and access; conflicting sources are flagged explicitly.

  • Specialists use only what you explicitly add to a task
  • AI-identified relations are labeled and never treated as confirmed facts
  • The first release is a read-only view; writing facts back is planned for phase two

Members, credits and policy, managed centrally

Admins manage members and departments, allocate business credits by department, and set which actions need approval, the extension allowlist and how far custom specialists can be shared.

  • Key actions are written to an audit log that can be exported for 90 days
  • Only business admins can see this page
  • Business-plan entitlements follow the official release

FAQ

Before we start working together

Where is data stored? Is it used to train models?
Personal information is stored in mainland China (Tencent Cloud, Shanghai region), and task content is never used to train models. Where a business contract says otherwise, the contract applies.
What do we need before implementation?
Agree on data sources, access scope, deliverables, acceptance and each side’s responsibilities, then start a controlled pilot with one business question.
Do we have to build a knowledge graph?
Not necessarily. We first check whether task inputs or configuration can solve the problem; a knowledge graph is not required for every project.
Will Sage change prices, purchase or publish on its own?
Sage acts only within the targets, scope and budget you authorize, never skips steps that need human confirmation, and you can withdraw authorization at any time.
How is pricing structured?
Subscriptions, implementation, model and resource usage, and ongoing maintenance are agreed separately, as set out in the contract.
Can Sage be deployed privately?
Business subscriptions do not include private deployment, unlimited customization or lifetime maintenance by default; if you need them, scope and cost are assessed in a dedicated implementation.
