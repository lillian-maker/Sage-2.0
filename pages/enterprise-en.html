Enterprise architecture

Five layers that bring AI into real operations

From data connections to results written back, every layer has clear boundaries, permissions and audit.

  1. 5Delivery

    Results are versioned and written back to business systems after acceptance; proven execution graphs are saved as Playbooks.

  2. 4Governance

    Authorization cards, budget limits, execution receipts and audit records; people always make the key decisions.

  3. 3Orchestration

    A lead specialist compiles an execution graph of specialist × skill × knowledge, runs steps in parallel and cross-checks them.

  4. 2Knowledge

    Business rules, metric definitions, process constraints and a knowledge graph; every fact carries its source, time and access rights.

  5. 1Connection

    Business systems such as ERP, CRM and HR, plus documents and reports, read through standard connections with authorization.

Each layer is delivered according to project scope and Sage app releases.

FAQ

Before we start working together

Where is data stored? Is it used to train models?
Personal information is stored in mainland China (Tencent Cloud, Shanghai region), and task content is never used to train models. Where a business contract says otherwise, the contract applies.
What do we need before implementation?
Agree on data sources, access scope, deliverables, acceptance and each side’s responsibilities, then start a controlled pilot with one business question.
Do we have to build a knowledge graph?
Not necessarily. We first check whether task inputs or configuration can solve the problem; a knowledge graph is not required for every project.
Will Sage change prices, purchase or publish on its own?
Sage acts only within the targets, scope and budget you authorize, never skips steps that need human confirmation, and you can withdraw authorization at any time.
How is pricing structured?
Subscriptions, implementation, model and resource usage, and ongoing maintenance are agreed separately, as set out in the contract.
Can Sage be deployed privately?
Business subscriptions do not include private deployment, unlimited customization or lifetime maintenance by default; if you need them, scope and cost are assessed in a dedicated implementation.
