What is Sage

Understand the product, service scope and operating boundaries.

Sage is an AI digital-colleague and multi-specialist workspace for global brand teams. You state a goal in business language; one lead digital specialist runs the task, bringing in other specialists, materials and tools within your authorization, and keeps evidence, progress, results and the decisions waiting for you in view. You can ask follow-ups, add materials, approve controlled actions and accept the results.

The standard product and enterprise services address different needs. The standard product provides shared business scenarios and working methods. Enterprise services define implementation scope and acceptance criteria around differences in company data, knowledge, scenarios and systems. Available features depend on the actual product release.

Product scope

Sage organizes work around eight core business scenarios and offers a Shopify operations entry. Scenario descriptions identify inputs, role responsibilities, typical deliverables and acceptance conditions. Examples are not evidence that real business actions have been executed.

  1. Existing GMV growth
  2. New-product evidence to commercial validation
  3. Supply planning, replenishment and delivery
  4. Market entry and compliant listings
  5. Customer journey and repeat purchase
  6. Business requirements to data products and tools
  7. Major quality and account incidents
  8. Business review and capability portfolio updates
  9. Shopify operations

Core concepts

  • Task: a collaboration record around one clear goal, holding the conversation, execution, results and acceptance.
  • Lead specialist: exactly one per task before it runs, responsible for breaking it down, organizing contributors and delivering; Sage Assistant leads when no specialist fits.
  • Contributors and reviewers: specialists who complete sub-goals under the lead or cross-check key conclusions.
  • Workspace: the container for materials, tasks and members, either personal or business.
  • Knowledge center: knowledge bases and the knowledge graph, providing sourced evidence for tasks.
  • Result: a versioned output of a task, such as a report, table, image or web page.
  • Extensions: skills, plugins, connectors and agents; installed, connected, authorized and enabled are separate states.
  • Automation: a scheduled rule; every run has its own record.
  • Receipt: the authoritative result of an external action; approvals, requests and execution results each have their own status.

Digital employees and human seats

Digital employees (called digital specialists in the Sage app) are AI roles with specialist responsibilities. Human seats are the accounts of people using the product. They are not the same billing or permission unit. Role names, avatars and configurations do not replace business authorization or prove production execution capability.

Enterprise services

Custom services cover data collection and governance, enterprise knowledge and knowledge graphs, scenario and product customization, and system integration. Before implementation, agree on sources, access scope, deliverables, acceptance and responsibilities. Knowledge graphs are not mandatory for every project.

Operating boundaries

Sage assists analysis and work; it does not replace the company’s responsibility for decisions and authorized actions. Reading public storefront pages does not grant administrative access. An action plan does not mean prices have changed, content has been published, purchases have been made or funds have moved. Missing inputs, conflicting facts or absent authorization require clarification or responsible human review.
