What is Sage

Understand the product, service scope and operating boundaries.

Sage is an AI-native decision and team collaboration tool for cross-border commerce and global brands. It organizes specialist roles around business questions, helping users assemble evidence, form judgments and propose actions while identifying execution that requires human approval.

The standard product and enterprise services address different needs. The standard product provides shared business scenarios and working methods. Enterprise services define implementation scope and acceptance criteria around differences in company data, knowledge, scenarios and systems. Available features depend on the actual product release.

Product scope

The website introduces eight core business scenarios and a Shopify operations entry. Scenario descriptions identify inputs, role responsibilities, typical deliverables and acceptance conditions. Examples are not evidence that real business actions have been executed.

  1. Existing GMV growth
  2. New-product evidence to commercial validation
  3. Supply planning, replenishment and delivery
  4. Market entry and compliant listings
  5. Customer journey and repeat purchase
  6. Business requirements to data products and tools
  7. Major quality and account incidents
  8. Business review and capability portfolio updates
  9. Shopify operations

Digital employees and human seats

Digital employees are AI roles with specialist responsibilities. Human seats are the accounts of people using the product. They are not the same billing or permission unit. Role names, avatars and configurations do not replace business authorization or prove production execution capability.

Enterprise services

Custom services cover data collection and governance, enterprise knowledge and knowledge graphs, scenario and product customization, and system integration. Before implementation, agree on sources, access scope, deliverables, acceptance and responsibilities. Knowledge graphs are not mandatory for every project.

Operating boundaries

Sage assists analysis and work; it does not replace the company’s responsibility for decisions and authorized actions. Reading public storefront pages does not grant administrative access. An action plan does not mean prices have changed, content has been published, purchases have been made or funds have moved. Missing inputs, conflicting facts or absent authorization require clarification or responsible human review.
