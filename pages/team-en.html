Closed loop

One task, a seven-step loop

One lead specialist owns the task, contributors work in parallel, and reviewers cross-check.
Every step is visible and adjustable, within budget and iteration limits.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Build a task ledger: goal, acceptance criteria, known facts, open questions and constraints.

  2. 02

    Assemble and compile

    Pick the lead and contributors on the specialist graph, then compile the steps into an execution graph with dependencies.

  3. 03

    Confirm the plan

    Complex tasks start with a plan card: steps, owners, estimated usage and the authorizations needed.

  4. 04

    Execute in parallel

    Independent steps run in parallel, and each step sees only the knowledge it needs and may access.

  5. 05

    Cross-check

    Each step checks itself against its acceptance criteria; key conclusions are cross-checked by a reviewer.

  6. 06

    Repair and replan

    Only failed steps are redone; with no progress or near the budget, Sage pauses and hands the decision to you.

  7. 07

    Deliver and retain

    Results arrive with sources for your acceptance; accepted facts flow back to the knowledge graph, and the execution graph can be saved as a Playbook.

Orchestration

Three graphs ground every orchestration

Each node in the execution graph = one specialist × one skill × the knowledge they are allowed to use

Who

Specialist graph

53 digital specialists and custom specialists, linked by common handoffs, upstream and downstream work, and review relationships. Used to assemble teams: simple tasks stay small, and key conclusions always get a reviewer.

How

Skill graph

Every skill has preconditions and outputs. Steps are compiled into an execution graph and checked for required connections and authorizations before running; a failed skill is replaced by an alternative.

On what basis

Knowledge graph

Business entities such as brands, products, markets, channels, competitors and regulations, with effective dates, sources and access rights. They ground each step and flag conflicting facts.

WHAT MAKES AN AI EMPLOYEE PROFESSIONAL

Expertise built into how every role works

Responsibility
Each role defines the decisions it owns, the materials it may use, the constraints it must check and its delivery standard.
Method
Reusable analysis steps and judgment rules, with versions, conditions of use and test samples.
Context
Understand this business first: positioning, markets, channels, assortment, funding constraints and past decisions.
Tools
Query and calculate through standard connections such as MCP to read verifiable business evidence; access is granted separately.
Review
Independently check sources, calculations, missing conditions and counterexamples, and return work or request more evidence.
Authorization
Advice and execution are authorized separately; anything touching funds, publishing or external systems needs your confirmation.

JUDGE THE WORK, NOT THE PERSONA

How to judge whether an AI team helped

Good work directly supports the next real decision.

Checkable evidence
Key conclusions identify sources, time, calculation definitions and applicability. Unconfirmed information remains explicitly unresolved.
Clear trade-offs
Explain alternatives, constraints, potential effects and unresolved disagreements, not just one recommendation.
Usable handoffs
The next owner knows what to validate, who confirms it and what to receive without researching the whole background again.
Distinct boundaries
Analysis, approval, execution and business improvement each have their own evidence.
