WHAT MAKES AN AI EMPLOYEE PROFESSIONAL

Professional judgment starts with methods and standards

Agents, Skills and MCP are implementation tools. What matters to the business is the judgment they support and the evidence they preserve.

01 / AGENT

Responsibilities shape the questions

A specialist role needs more than a name: it defines the decision, permitted information, required constraint checks and deliverable standards. Opportunity research first tests the evidence; commercial judgment then asks whether the supported opportunity merits investment.

Every role earns its place through acceptance on real tasks, not through a title alone.

02 / SKILLS

Reusable methods, reliable results

Skills give roles reusable analytical steps, judgment rules and output requirements. VOC research checks samples, demand frequency, alternatives and contrary evidence, not just sentiment. Business analysis checks definitions and constraints, not just growth rates.

Every method has a version, conditions of use and test samples, and each improvement is verified on the same samples.

03 / CONTEXT

Understand the company before applying industry knowledge

Positioning, markets, channels, product mix, financial constraints and prior decisions form the business context. General industry knowledge cannot replace these conditions; the same recommendation may have opposite effects for different brands.

Shared context preserves sources, dates and permissions, separating facts, inferences and missing information so outdated experience is not mistaken for current evidence.

04 / MCP

Tools provide evidence; authority is granted separately

MCP standardizes tool and data connections. Record queries, calculations and retrieval ground judgments in checkable business evidence rather than model memory alone.

Data accuracy, access, execution permission and actual results are each confirmed separately; price changes, purchases and publishing each need your explicit authorization.

05 / HANDOFF

Handoff work that others can use

Every handoff tells the next role what it needs: where the evidence comes from, what it covers, which assumptions remain untested and when the decision can proceed.

Handoff standards determine reusability. Clear sources, constraints and unresolved questions matter more than a longer chat history.

06 / REVIEW

Review needs a distinct checking responsibility

Reviewers carry their own checking duties: they verify sources, calculations, missing conditions, counterexamples and authorization, and can return work or request more evidence.

Important conclusions are also confirmed with external evidence, deterministic checks and human judgment.

07 / CONTROL

Advice and execution are authorized separately

Proposing, approving, executing and confirming completion have distinct responsibilities. Financial actions, publishing and external system changes require an owner, budget and revocation conditions.

AI proposes, the business authorizes, and real business results show whether a goal was met.

08 / EVALUATION

Expertise must withstand repeated evaluation

Acceptance checks factual accuracy, calculation definitions, sufficient evidence, constraints and usable handoffs. After each version change, the same tasks are compared again so reliability rests on sustained results.

Evaluation drives continuous method improvement, so every upgrade is backed by evidence.

COLLABORATION WITH A REASON

Every judgment
stands up to cross-disciplinary scrutiny

Collaborate where judgments influence one another

When demand evidence, product feasibility, profitability and entry risks constrain one another, specialist research maintains depth. Synthesis should preserve disagreement, identify conditions and evidence gaps, and name the person who must confirm them.

Organize roles around the question, not run every employee at once. Research may proceed in parallel; dependent work needs explicit acceptance conditions.

Keep simple work simple

Field validation, calculations, a single retrieval or short copy may need only a rule, tool or one role. Extra time and Sage Credits are worthwhile only when collaboration improves coverage, review quality or handoff reliability.

Sage builds each team around the task, with the combination of roles that fits it best.

JUDGE THE WORK, NOT THE PERSONA

How to judge whether an AI team helped

Good work directly supports the next real decision.

Checkable evidence
Key conclusions identify sources, time, calculation definitions and applicability. Unconfirmed information remains explicitly unresolved.
Clear trade-offs
Explain alternatives, constraints, potential effects and unresolved disagreements, not just one recommendation.
Usable handoffs
The next owner knows what to validate, who confirms it and what to receive without researching the whole background again.
Distinct boundaries
Analysis, approval, execution and business improvement each have their own evidence.
